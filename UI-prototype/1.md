## Brand & Style

This design system is built on a foundation of **Minimalism** blended with **Soft Neomorphism**. It evokes a feeling of creative energy, clarity, and approachability. The target audience includes creative professionals and digital product builders who value high-signal environments that don't sacrifice playfulness.

The aesthetic utilizes expansive whitespace and a "soft-UI" approach where elements feel physically integrated into the surface. The mood is optimistic and fresh, driven by a balance of clinical layout precision and warm, vibrant accent colors.

## Colors

The palette is centered around a vibrant violet-purple that serves as the primary action color. Secondary accents of soft peach and mint green are used sparingly to indicate variety or status without overwhelming the visual hierarchy.

- **Primary:** High-saturation purple for main actions and active states.
- **Secondary/Accents:** Peach and mint for data visualization and subtle highlights.
- **Neutrals:** A clean, cool-toned off-white background paired with pure white surfaces.
- **Typography:** High-contrast charcoal for headings to ensure readability, with a softer gray for long-form body text.

## Typography

The design system utilizes **Plus Jakarta Sans** for its geometric yet friendly characteristics. Headings are bold and impactful, featuring tight letter-spacing and generous line heights to maintain an editorial feel. 

Body text remains legible with increased line-height to match the airy aesthetic of the layout. Links and interactive labels often use a semi-bold weight to distinguish them from static content.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a **Fixed Grid** model for desktop and a **Fluid Grid** for mobile devices. It relies on a 12-column system with generous gutters to prevent visual crowding.

Spacing is aggressive; the "medium" unit (32px) is the standard for internal card padding, while the "large" unit (64px+) is used to separate distinct content sections. This breathing room is essential to achieving the premium, minimal feel of the design system.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is communicated through **Ambient Shadows** and **Tonal Layering**. Surfaces do not appear to "float" high above the background; instead, they feel like soft extrusions or layers resting just above the base.

- **Level 1 (Cards):** Very soft, wide-dispersion shadows with low opacity (approx 4-6%) using a slight tint of the primary color or neutral navy.
- **Level 2 (Hover):** Increased shadow spread and a slight vertical lift.
- **Interactive Elements:** Subtle inner shadows or "pressed" states are used for active toggles to reinforce the tactile nature of the UI.

## Shapes

The shape language is defined by smooth, organic curves. 
- **Buttons:** Use a pill-shaped (fully rounded) radius to make them appear friendly and distinct from layout containers.
- **Cards/Containers:** Use a medium-large radius (16px - 24px) to soften the perimeter of the content.
- **Inputs:** Follow the card radius for a cohesive, nested appearance.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Pill-shaped, solid primary purple with white text.
- **Secondary:** Light gray background or subtle outline with charcoal text.
- **Ghost:** No background or border, utilizing bold primary-colored text.

### Cards
Cards are the primary container. They feature white backgrounds, subtle 1px borders in a very light gray (#EAECEF), and soft ambient shadows. Internal padding should never drop below 24px.

### Inputs
Minimalist style with a soft-gray background (#F1F3F5) and no heavy borders. On focus, the border transitions to the primary purple with a subtle outer glow.

### Tables & Lists
Tables should be extremely "airy," removing vertical dividers entirely. Use only light horizontal rules and ample row height (64px+) to maintain the clean aesthetic.

### Toggles & Sliders
Use the primary color for active states. Sliders should feature a round, oversized handle to emphasize the tactile, playful nature of the design system.
